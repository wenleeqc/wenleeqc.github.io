User Flow

Color Palette

Warm colors are used to convey a sense of fun and
playfulness. Red and green are chosen to invoke
“appleliness.” Cream is used as a base to complement the
bold colors within the palette.

Typography

Roboto Serif is used for display text and CTAs. Its curved
letterforms invoke warmth and brings some levity to a serious
topic. Roboto is used as the main system font. It is easy to
read on mobile screens and complements Roboto Serif.
